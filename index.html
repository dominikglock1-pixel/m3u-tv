Android & iOS · In development

The same idea. On the go.

Mobile versions are in development. A discreet ad in the free home menu and a separate one-time Premium purchase of around €3 per platform are planned. Premium purchases are not active yet.

Add. Find. Watch.

Add a playlist using provider details, an HTTP/HTTPS address, a file or pasted text. Search your library, browse groups and switch between TV, movies and series.

Available content and playback depend on your playlist, media formats and device.

A project in development

Public store availability has not been announced. Samsung TV testing is ongoing; mobile device testing is still ahead. Supported TV model groups will be confirmed before release.

M3U TV does not provide channels, films, series or streaming subscriptions. Use your own playlist and make sure you have the necessary rights.
